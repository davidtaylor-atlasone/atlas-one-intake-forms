CSV or Excel format: columns are Emp Seq #, EE #, Last name, First name, Relationship, Date of birth, Gender, Street, City, State, ZIP, Coverage tier, Hire date, Employee status, Position, Pay type, Salary or rate, SSN. Relationship is Employee, Spouse, Child or Domestic partner; a spouse or child row belongs to the employee row above it. Tier accepts EE, EE+Spouse, EE+Child(ren), Family, or Waiving. Hire date, Employee status, Position, Pay type and Salary or rate apply to employee rows only. Salary or rate is required on an employee row when Accident, Hospital indemnity, Short term disability or Long term disability is requested. The older eight column template (with Tobacco and Dependents) still imports. SSN is optional: it is masked on screen, saved only into the file you download, and never sent anywhere by this page.
