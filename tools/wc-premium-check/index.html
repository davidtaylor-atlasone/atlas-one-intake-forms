How auditors treat subcontractors (NCCI Basic Manual Rule 2)

  • Insured sub — valid COI / WC certificate on file: excluded from your audit. Adds $0.
  • Uninsured sub — no COI (default): charged on 100% of what you paid them, unless you provide invoices splitting labor vs. materials.
  • Uninsured, labor only (documented): not less than 90% of the amount.
  • Uninsured, labor + materials (invoices separating them): not less than 50% (auditor removes materials, capped at 50%).
  • Uninsured, mobile equipment with operator doing all the work: about 33% (one-third).
The reliable way to $0 is collecting the COI — the material reductions require documentation most subs never provide.
